## A. Formal model
Inputs are immutable local UTF-8 Writer drafts with at most 128 KiB and 2,048 normalized lines; a user explicitly chooses plain or formatted DOCX copy and may separately choose the new restricted numbered-line interpretation. Output is a new bounded DOCX, not an overwrite of the source. For every accepted draft and selected mode, generate one immutable copy or refuse the whole conversion; for every default-mode input preserve the R57 output behavior. In the opt-in mode, only documented `1. ` prefixes with following content become level-zero numbered paragraphs. Unmatched/ambiguous inline markers remain literal. Numbering is a DOCX numbering instance; the output does not assert independent restart of separated groups, exact rendering, arbitrary Markdown, nesting or rich editing. For a bounded rectangular text grid, the independent transpose option maps source `(row,column)` to destination `(column,row)` at the chosen anchor after parsing; it preserves quotedness, blank clearing and decimal/formula-safety classifications, and checks both source and destination dimensions. Formulas, bytes, lifecycle and provider conditions in the spreadsheet remain governed by R59/R22 contracts.

The access model includes source, bounded inputs, independently produced ZIP/XML/python-docx inspection and an API34 emulator; it excludes hidden Office engines, production provider identities, physical devices and omniscient concurrency observations. Resources include UTF-8 bytes, lines, text runs, XLSX cells, XML nodes and the existing five-second worker lifetime. ∀ admitted local inputs the implementation must satisfy its grammar and atomicity; ∃ finite executable cases and independent readers provide evidence but do not prove ∀ external devices, providers or Office programs. Cancellation must not apply the reviewed operation, a stale formatted-copy or grid confirmation must not authorize a different draft, and defaults must not silently become either opt-in mode. The older plain-copy confirmation path is not newly revision-bound by this work.

## C. Research frontier
[Microsoft's NumberingId reference](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/documentformat.openxml.wordprocessing.numberingid?view=openxml-3.0.1) states that `w:numId` selects a numbering-definition instance; [NumberingProperties](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/documentformat.openxml.wordprocessing.numberingproperties?view=openxml-3.0.1) associates a paragraph with `numId` and `ilvl`. A Microsoft Q&A discussion offers conflicting advice about restart behavior across instances; it is not an interoperability proof. This implementation therefore discloses shared-instance behavior rather than promising arbitrary restart semantics, and does not adopt outside code. [Android's Binder DeathRecipient reference](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/os/IBinder.DeathRecipient) says the callback observes process death, not why the process died. At R59 the death near the 5-second watchdog is consistent with its limit but could have another cause. [Android's AGP 9.1.1 notes](https://developer.android.com/build/releases/agp-9-1-0-release-notes) specify Gradle 9.3.1/JDK17 and API37.0 support; [Studio compatibility](https://developer.android.com/build/releases/about-agp) lists Rabbit 1 support for AGP7.1–9.4. This is documentation compatibility, not an observed Studio GUI run. [Microsoft's transpose description](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/office/vba/api/excel.worksheetfunction.transpose) maps source rows to output columns; OSB's bounded text-grid operation does not claim Excel's formatting or formula-paste behavior. [openpyxl's reader documentation](https://openpyxl.readthedocs.io/en/3.1/api/openpyxl.reader.excel.html) distinguishes formula text from saved cached values, not recalculation. The separately installed readers have permissive published licenses ([openpyxl MIT/Expat](https://openpyxl.readthedocs.io/en/3.0/), [python-docx MIT](https://github.com/python-openxml/python-docx/blob/master/LICENSE)); they are host-only alternatives, not bundled/copied app code. [Android's adb documentation](https://developer.android.com/tools/adb) defines `offline` as disconnected or not responding, not a causal diagnosis for the failed full-suite run. General DOCX fidelity and physical/provider/security acceptance remain open.

The default-preservation and `2. `, `01. `, nested/empty-marker adversarial cases must fail such an implementation.
