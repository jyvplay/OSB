Exact frontier: Android grants scoped isolation/capability mechanisms; finite tests can establish particular executions. Tails/Tor document observer and identity boundaries, not proof that every privacy improvement is impossible. A complete original browser/Office/system design still needs genuine engines, callback/resource containment, licensing, device/AX/fidelity matrices and comparable measured footprint. These open interfaces are not closed by our conditional Z3 models.
