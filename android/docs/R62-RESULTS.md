## A. Model, quantifiers and limits
An admissible sheet is the existing immutable ≤256-cell, A1:Z100, revisioned single-sheet draft. A reviewed rectangle has at most 256 address positions and all coordinates within that grid. Clear removes all populated contents inside it, preserving outside cells. Literal fill copies an existing nonempty TEXT/NUMBER/BOOLEAN source cell identically into each position, never converts formula-looking text into a formula, and refuses a FORMULA source. Under default protection, any existing nonidentical destination refuses the entire fill; explicit overwrite requires a separate review. A zero-change candidate preserves identity and revision and creates no undo step; a nonzero candidate validates/recalculates the whole result before a single revision/undo step. Consent binds the exact source draft instance and selected source; stale or canceled previews apply nothing. The Writer extension recognizes only documented nonnested double-tilde pairs; old methods and opt-out output remain byte-identical. For **every admitted** input the contract is intended; **finite observed** corpus and device cases provide evidence, not proof over all inputs or providers. Budgets are UTF-8 bytes, text runs, addresses, revisions and actual provider I/O, not claims of time/energy benchmarks. Different problems include full rich editing, adjusted formula fill, cross-sheet ranges, styles, Office fidelity, privacy certification and physical-device acceptance.

## B. Outcome space
H+: these explicitly bounded operations can be implemented and tested. H−: no proof that full original Office or the overall product is impossible is asserted; finite emulators cannot establish universal compatibility. H∂: malformed/oversize text, formula source, overwritten formulas, revision exhaustion, stale consent, worker limits, viewport/font and provider behavior change the answer.

## C. Research frontier
[Microsoft's `w:strike` definition](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/documentformat.openxml.wordprocessing.strike?view=openxml-3.0.1) identifies an on/off run property, not a rendering test. [Excel ClearContents](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/office/vba/api/excel.range.clearcontents) clears formulas and values while retaining formatting; this draft has no formatting to retain. [Excel formula fill](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/fill-a-formula-down-into-adjacent-cells-041edfe2-05bc-40e6-b933-ef48c3f308c6) adjusts relative references; the proposed literal-only fill intentionally refuses formulas and is not an Excel clone. Python readers and the documented standard are independent inspection aids, not Office acceptance. The open interface includes actual Word/Excel behavior, real Android rendering and diverse providers.

## D. Negative space and modal shortcut
Do not silently enable strike; delete unmatched markers; interpret nesting as supported; count a `w:strike` tag as a rendered glyph; modify source text; compare only revision while accepting a different draft; treat zero-length text as a blank without disclosure; apply a stale preview; add an undo step for a no-op; fill formula text as a shifted reference; copy `=...` text as a formula; overwrite an occupied cell under default protection; partially apply a later-conflicting range; bypass 256 positions by having few occupied cells; permit reversed/out-of-sheet ranges without checking; change outside cells; lose type; skip recalculation; accept revision overflow; count host XML as Android; assume provider atomicity; call a later CI pass a diagnosis; or conflate 50 testable subsets with 50 completed modules. The tempting shortcut copies a source's raw string with a default TEXT kind to every destination and mutates as it goes. It loses numeric/Boolean types, risks partial overwrite and mishandles formula-like text. Attacks must include type-preserving round trips, a late occupied conflict, formula-source refusal, source identity/selection staleness and original-map equality after refusal.

## E–G. Six mechanisms, artifacts and strongest adversaries
1. Conservative opt-in lexer: actual DOCX and exact default-byte comparison; attack unmatched, escaped, nested, adjacent and triple delimiters. Full Markdown is the open/global interface.
2. Rectangle admission: normalized coordinates and 256-position cap; attack reversed bounds, Unicode digits, one-over and Z100. Local gap: defined finite grammar.
3. Immutable transaction: validated candidate and single revision; attack late conflict, revision max, byte/cell caps and mutation aliasing. Local gap: external persistence.
4. Typed literal fill: copy TEXT/NUMBER/BOOLEAN only; attack leading zeros, `=1+1` text, same input/different type, formula source and empty source. Formula-reference rewriting is deliberately excluded/global.
5. Snapshot-bound review: real view model cancel/accept/undo; attack changed selection/draft, reopening and no-op. Android lifecycle/provider behavior is the external gap.
6. Independent readers and mutation tests: actual ZIP/XML/python-docx/openpyxl files; attack missing parts, changed type, added formula, wrong coordinate or stale file. Office rendering is the external gap.
7. Host and Android gates: standalone Kotlin compile, clean checkout, pinned predecessors, real emulator tests and inspected native frames. Attack missing dependencies and partial workflow success; hardware/OEM evidence remains open.
